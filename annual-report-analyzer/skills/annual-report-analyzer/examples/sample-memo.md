## Executive Summary

**Hold.** Example Corp is a high-quality operator whose shares already reflect its strengths. Revenue grew 8% and operating margin expanded to 21% [F, Item 7], but the stock trades at 26x forward earnings [W, illustrative source, 2026-09-28], a premium to its five-year average.

> Key takeaway: a good business at a full price. We would become buyers below roughly $90 or on evidence that margins can exceed 23%.

## Company Information
